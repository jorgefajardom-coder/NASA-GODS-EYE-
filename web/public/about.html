NASA Space Apps 2026 · Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map · team Houston, We Have a Bug!

Status: REPLAY of a simulated mission. The terrain heights and the draped image are real NASA/USGS products; the mission, agent motion, budgets, observations and points of interest are simulated or synthetic. Not a live mission and not certified for real operations. Open the 3D viewer.

What it shows

A saved expedition of an astronaut, a rover and a drone over a 2 km crop of Jezero crater. In the demo run the drone surveys an uncertain corridor, finds a scarp, the first route is invalidated and the astronaut is replanned around it, reaches goal B and returns to base. The viewer draws what the mission recorded; it never plans routes itself.

  • Terrain layers: Orbital map (prior), Updated map (prior plus observations, by map version), Slope, Uncertainty.
  • Routes: the route in force with its status, and invalidated routes kept visible (dashed) for comparison.
  • Agents and resources: poses, modes, oxygen, energy and time, in a details panel and an agent dial with a heading-up minimap.
  • Ground layers tab: USGS SIM 3464 geologic units draped on the terrain and a schematic cut-away (not measured stratigraphy).
  • Atmos tab: historical MEDA summaries at Jezero and an estimated crew dose from the MSL RAD Gale surface rate; not current weather.
  • First person: the view from the astronaut, rover or drone, with compass, site bearings, resources, the route ahead and the next waypoint.
  • Drone camera scan: redraws the existing orbital slope around the drone in display bands; not a new observation.

Limits

  • The slope averages about 60 m of ground per value: a lower bound for local slope, not a hazard map.
  • Height uncertainty in the demo is assumed (set by hand), not measured.
  • Vertical exaggeration (2x by default) is visual only.
  • Scan bands and resource thresholds are display choices awaiting science approval.
  • Integrated mission acceptance is still pending; no result here is mission evidence.

Machine-readable access

  • llms.txt: this information in Markdown for language models.
  • replay/demo/manifest.json, timeline.json and routes.json: the demo run.
  • In the running viewer, window.mge.state() returns the current replay state as JSON.

AI use

AI tools were used and are declared, with the affected files, in AI_USAGE.md and ai_usage.json. The intro video (Kling) and the astronaut portrait (ChatGPT) are AI-generated and carry a visible "AI-generated" watermark.
