Mars Gods Eye — Mission Viewer

NASA Space Apps 2026 · Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map · team Houston, We Have a Bug!

A 3D viewer that replays a saved, simulated expedition of an astronaut, a rover and a drone over a 2 km crop of real NASA/USGS CTX terrain at Jezero crater, Mars. It shows the orbital terrain prior and the map updated by observations, height uncertainty, slope, proposed and invalidated routes, replanning, resources, geology and historical environment references.

Status: REPLAY of simulated data. Only the terrain heights and the draped image are NASA/USGS data; the mission, budgets, observations and points of interest are simulated or synthetic. It is not a live mission and not certified for real operations.
