## Review and synchronization

The source package requests a PR, owner review and visual checks before updating the separate Wiki repository. This edition is prepared for that review. Its comparison preserves the measured/model-derived figures, references, formulas and recorded analytical case.

The [edition manifest](audit/editorial-20261115/manifest.json) records selection and exclusion for all 20 inputs. The original package guidance is retained as [sealed instructions](audit/editorial-20261115/PROMPT_FOR_CODEX.md.sealed) and [sealed usage matrix](audit/editorial-20261115/ASSET_USAGE_MATRIX.md.sealed). These are provenance documents; article conclusions follow their scientific sources.

PNG and WebP variants have the same conceptual content. The WebP files are the inline reading copies, with the footer retained. Full-size images support reading detailed labels; the underlying numerical examples remain available in the original deterministic figures.
