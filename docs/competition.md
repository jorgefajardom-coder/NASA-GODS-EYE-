- [ ] All members are confirmed in the same event and challenge.
- [ ] Project page and linked material are in English.
- [ ] Demo: video at most 30 seconds OR deck at most 7 slides.
- [ ] Final project and demo links work publicly without passwords.
- [ ] Static replay works with ROS/Gazebo/gateway unavailable.
- [ ] Eligible NASA resources are used/cited for global judging; science plan
      additionally requires meaningful MRO and MGS contributions.
- [ ] Actual product IDs, scales, licenses and checksums are recorded.
- [ ] Prior work/third-party reuse has documented eligibility and permission.
- [ ] AI-generated code/data/audio is disclosed in text and metadata.
- [ ] AI-generated images/videos carry a visible `AI-generated` watermark.
- [ ] Generated content contains no NASA branding/design elements.
- [ ] No submitted image/audio/video includes people under 18, per team guide.
- [ ] Simulation, replay, hypothetical resources and uncertainty are labeled.
- [ ] No physical safety certification or unmeasured performance is claimed.
- [ ] Local organizer notified with package revision/links/time and required
      lead time; contact details remain private (JES-07/AC-10).
- [ ] Explicit organizer endorsement and required corrections recorded for
      the final package before submission; silence is not approval.
- [ ] Form is reviewed and submitted before the applicable cutoff.
- [ ] Actual Submit for Judging confirmation retained with final revision.
